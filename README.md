# RLCA Academy

**A Christian-based entrepreneurial homeschooling program.**

## What is RLCA Academy?

RLCA Academy is a homeschooling initiative built around Christian values, academic learning, and entrepreneurial thinking. Its goal is to help students develop a strong educational foundation while learning how to think independently, solve problems, build practical skills, and understand the fundamentals of business and entrepreneurship.

The program is designed to bring faith, education, personal development, and real-world preparation together in a structured learning experience.

## Core Program Foundations

- **Christian education:** Integrate biblical principles, Christian values, character development, and faith into the educational journey.
- **Academic learning:** Establish a structured foundation for homeschooling, with organized learning materials and educational activities.
- **Entrepreneurship:** Introduce business fundamentals, creative thinking, problem-solving, financial awareness, and the process of turning ideas into practical ventures.
- **Practical skill development:** Encourage independent thinking, responsibility, communication, decision-making, and hands-on learning.
- **Personal growth:** Help students build discipline, confidence, leadership, and a sense of purpose as they prepare for life beyond the classroom.

## Platform Goals

RLCA Academy V2 is being rebuilt to establish a foundation for a digital platform that can support the program's educational goals.

The intended direction includes organizing learning resources, structuring educational content, supporting student learning and progress, and providing a foundation for future tools that serve the academy's needs.

## Technology Foundation

The platform's planned technology stack includes:

- **React** for building the user interface
- **Vite** for development and application builds
- **Supabase** for backend services and data infrastructure
- **Vercel** for deployment
- **GitHub** for source control and project development

## Current Status

RLCA Academy V2 is in its rebuild and planning stage. The current focus is establishing the minimum viable product (MVP) and defining the core functionality needed to support the academy's vision.

The long-term goal is to create a flexible educational platform that supports Christian-centered homeschooling, entrepreneurial development, and practical preparation for the future.
